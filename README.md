# Adb-Manager
Adb Manager was build for android devinces , it allows you to transfer files and download them from and to the device ,
it inspects installed apckages and it uses AI in case you want to look for information regarding to the packages
installed and what they do in your device , network connections monitor in the device and others , this app was build
initially with Openai and finished with claude from anthropic due to free tier limitations in openai regarding to code .
More detailed data of the app will arrive soon .
